Privacy Policy — Arenas of Origins

Last updated: November 26, 2026. Version française

In short

  • No account. The game works without an account and never asks for your name, email or phone number.
  • The game itself collects no personal data. Your progress stays on your phone.
  • Online play: a relay server technically sees your IP address. It is used to run the match and protect the server; it is not used to profile you.
  • Advertising: the game offers optional rewarded videos through Google AdMob, with your consent in Europe (TCF framework). AdMob may process device identifiers.
  • Former website waiting list: sign-ups are suspended. Only the email you provided to be notified of the release was collected.
  • Delete your data: write to arenedesorigines@gmail.com.

1. Who is responsible

Publisher: the Arenas of Origins team, independent publisher of the game « Arènes des Origines » (Android and iOS app, com.amar.arenedesorigines) and of the website https://arenesdesorigines.github.io.

Contact for any question or request: arenedesorigines@gmail.com

2. What stays on your device

Your progress (floors, unlocked fighters and skins, your created fighter, coins, settings, challenges, game statistics) is saved only on your phone. It is not sent to us. It is erased if you uninstall the game, clear its data in your phone settings (Android) or delete the app (iOS).

3. Online play (relay server)

Local Wi-Fi connects devices directly on your network: nothing goes through us. To play at a distance, the game connects to a relay server that brings players together.

  • What passes through the relay: your nickname (choose one that does not reveal your identity), a random player identifier created by the game, in-game actions during the match and, between friends (feature in testing), chat messages and challenges. A message for a disconnected friend is kept only until delivery.
  • Your IP address: like any internet server, the relay technically sees it, because no connection is possible without it. It is used only to run the match and limit abuse. It is not used to profile you, never sold, and never combined with other data. It may appear in the server's technical logs, kept at most 6 months for security and bug fixing.
  • Hosting: the relay is hosted by a hosting provider (currently Render Services, Inc., United States). This page will be updated if the host changes.
  • Legal basis: the service you request (playing online) and legitimate interest (security).

3 bis. Daily Fight leaderboard

This feature is coming in a future version of the game.

  • When you win the Daily Fight, the game sends our server your nickname (from your Profile, or « CombattantNNNN » by default), your score, the number of remaining lives, the fight duration, the date, the game version and an anonymous identifier created by the game on your phone. This identifier is neither your phone number nor an account: it is only used to keep your best result of the day, with one row per player.
  • This data is used only to display the day's leaderboard (the top 50 and your rank). Your nickname and score are visible to other players.
  • We do not collect any email, real name, phone number or location, and nothing is used for advertising. Your IP address is not stored: it is used for a few minutes, in memory, to limit abuse.
  • Leaderboards are automatically erased within 30 days at most.
  • Without a connection, nothing is sent: your record stays on your phone only.
  • To have your result deleted earlier, write to arenedesorigines@gmail.com with your nickname and the date.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded video ads (rewarded with coins), provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC), never during a fight. No other advertising is forced on you.

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a Google consent message (compliant with the IAB TCF framework) first asks for your choice: accept, refuse or customize. If you refuse, the game remains fully playable. You can change your mind at any time in the game settings (« Privacy » button).
  • What Google may process: device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID), IP address, device model, language, approximate country and interactions with ads, in order to display and measure ads, fight fraud and, only if you accept, personalize ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google > Ads).
  • On iPhone / iPad: iOS asks for your permission before any cross-app ad tracking (« App Tracking Transparency » prompt, IDFA). If you refuse, ads are still shown but without personalized tracking. You can change this choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your bank card, name or email: Google only sends us what is needed to deliver your purchase (product, purchase token, order number, date, status). Purchases never make any fighter stronger.

6. Downloaded content

The game downloads packs (cutscenes, voices) from GitHub and its jsDelivr content delivery network. As with any download, these services receive your IP address. No other data is sent.

7. Website and waiting list

  • The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which keeps technical logs (IP address) for security. The site uses neither tracking cookies nor analytics.
  • Former waiting list: sign-ups are suspended. The form used FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to forward to arenedesorigines@gmail.com only the email you chose to give in order to be notified of the game's release. It is never sold or shared. It is deleted on simple request, or at the latest 3 years after our last exchange. Legal basis: your consent.
  • If you write to us by email or on social networks, your message is kept for the time needed to answer you.

8. Transfers outside the European Union

Google, GitHub, the relay host and FormSubmit may process data in the United States or elsewhere, on the basis of the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for certified companies, or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

9. Children

The game never knowingly asks for or collects identity data, including from children. Chat only works between added friends. Ads are requested from Google with settings suited to the game's audience. If you are a parent and believe your child sent us data (for example their email), write to us: we will delete it.

10. What we do not do

No selling of data. No account. No profiling. No access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.

11. Your rights and deleting your data

Under the GDPR, you can access your data, correct it, erase it, object to its use, retrieve it and withdraw your consent at any time.

For a deletion request (for example your waiting-list email): write to arenedesorigines@gmail.com with the subject « Delete my data ». Answer within one month. Your in-game progress is erased by uninstalling the app. For advertising and purchases, you can also act from your Google account.

If you are not satisfied, you can contact the CNIL: cnil.fr/fr/plaintes

12. Changes

This policy may change, for example if a service is added. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version.
