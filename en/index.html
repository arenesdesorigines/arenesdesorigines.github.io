The gameClimb the Tower

100 floors, only one champion. Work your way up the Tower in Story Mode, take on 29 fighters across 12 hand-painted arenas, rack up coins, and create your own fighter. Local Wi-Fi multiplayer is coming soon. Available in French, English, Spanish, German, Italian, and Portuguese.

The rosterAll 29 Fighters

Each fighter has their own style, range, four special moves, and combos (six to eight per fighter, including one signature combo). The eight bars show what you see on the fighter select screen: speed, damage, defense, specials, jump, range, weight, and attack rate. Thirteen fighters are unlocked from the start; get sixteen more in the Shop, and unlock fourteen of those by beating them in the Story Mode Tower. Each profile also tells their story in the Tower. Tap a fighter to see their profile.

Gameplay in ActionFight, Climb, Create

A 2D brawler inspired by platform fighters: battle on floating stages, rack up damage as a percent, and win by blasting rivals out of the arena. Every screenshot is straight from the game.

Story Mode: The Tower

Climb a 100-floor Tower packed with cutscenes. There's a boss every 10 floors and wild rule twists on each one: time limit, tracking laser, low gravity, wandering spirit, sudden death, meteor shower, wind, slippery floor. After every boss, pick one of three Tower Blessings.

VS the AI

Challenge the computer with any fighter you want—from easy to brutal—across all 12 Arenas in the game.

Game Modes

Ranked, Survival, Tournament (quarterfinals, semifinals, final: win three matches in a row to be champ), and Fight of the Day. Plus daily and weekly missions for coins.

29 fighters

Each has four special moves (neutral, side, up, down) and unique combos. Thirteen are playable from the start, the rest unlock in the Tower or Shop.

Coins, and soon Crystals

Earn coins in battles and spend them in the Shop. Coming soon: Crystals—rare purple currency from the Tower, achievements, challenges, and gold chests. Collect them for exclusive Crystal Collection items. They’re dropping in an upcoming update.

Play with friends over local Wi-Fi

Coming soon. Two phones on the same Wi-Fi, or two players on one phone. For now, everything else is single-player.

Touch controls

  • Joystick: move and aim (up to jump).
  • ATK: attack. On the ground, a three-hit combo (even while walking); in the air, attack based on your direction.
  • CHARGE: hold to power up, release to hit.
  • SPEC + direction: four special moves (neutral, side, up, down).
  • JUMP (twice for double jump). SHIELD: hold to block, add a direction to dodge. GRAB: grab your opponent when super close.
  • Pause at the top center.

GuideTips and strategies

The controls, at a glance

Joystick to move, ATK for a 3-hit combo, CHARGE (hold then release) for a heavy hit, SPEC + direction triggers your four specials, JUMP twice for a double jump, SHIELD to block, and GRAB if you’re up close.

Learn your combos

From the select screen, open “Attacks”: every fighter’s got six to eight combos, with a signature finisher. Tap the moves in order, don’t stall.

Perfect guard

Raise your shield right as a hit connects: that’s a perfect guard, so you can immediately counter. Just holding shield isn’t enough; timing is key.

Shield + direction: dodge

Hold SHIELD and push the joystick to dodge. But don’t turtle too long — overused shields break eventually.

The AI adapts: mix up attacks

Spam the same move five times straight (or nine times for basic attacks) and your opponent will level up for about eight seconds. Switch between shots, combos, and specials.

Get back on stage

SPEC + up is every fighter’s recovery: Omega’s Jetpack, Vesper’s Shadow Flip, Ashgar’s Eruption, Mortrek’s Spectral Flight, Eldrin’s Storm Hover... Save your double jump for last (Zephyr can even triple jump).

See the 7 other tips

Read the tower floor rules

In the Tower, certain floors switch things up: 75-second timers, tracking lasers, low gravity, wandering spirits, sudden death (one life only), meteor showers, wind, or slippery ground. Check the floor conditions before you dive in.

Tower Blessings

After each guardian you defeat (one every 10 floors), pick a Blessing out of three. It lasts till the next guardian — only in Story Mode, never for purchase.

Edges and walls

Jump into a ledge or wall to grab on — sometimes that’s the only thing keeping you in the fight after you get launched.

Punish recovery lag

Big moves leave fighters open for a sec. Use that window for a combo.

Versus long-range fighters

Don’t just back up against Kaldrin and Nova — they hit from afar: either jump in, or close the gap with Shadow’s Ghost Dash or Drak’s Dive.

Unlocking fighters

Thirteen fighters are unlocked from the start. Beat the others in the Tower to unlock them, or grab them in the Shop. Shadow and Drak are only unlocked from the Shop.

Daily and weekly missions

Five daily and five weekly missions earn you coins: hit up the Missions menu before you play.

Frequently AskedFAQ

How do I install the game?

It’s coming soon to Google Play: you’ll install it just like any other Android app. It’s not available yet. Sign up in the Notify me section to get notified as soon as it drops.

Which platforms?

Android, via Google Play, coming soon.

Which languages?

French, English, Spanish, German, Italian, and Portuguese.

Do I need an account?

Nope! No name, email, or account needed to play.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone: floors cleared, stats, unlocked fighters, custom fighter, and settings. Uninstalling the app wipes everything.

How does Story Mode work?

Climb the Tower of Origins, floor by floor, all the way to the 100. You'll face a guardian every 10 floors, and some floors shake up the rules (timed matches, tracking lasers, low gravity, wandering spirit, sudden death, meteors, wind, or slippery ground). After each guardian, pick one of three Tower Blessings.

What are Crystals?

They're a second, purple currency—rarer than coins, coming soon : they'll land in a future update along with the exclusive Crystal Collection.

How do I unlock fighters?

Thirteen are unlocked from the start. Fourteen more unlock by beating them in the Tower (floor 40 to 95); all unlockable fighters, including Shadow and Drak, are also available in the Shop.

What about multiplayer?

Playing friends over local Wi-Fi (two phones on the same Wi-Fi, or two players on one phone) is marked "Coming soon" in-game: expect it in a future update.

How do I pull off a combo?

On the select screen, tap "Attacks" to see that fighter's four specials and combos (like Hit 1, Hit 2, Up Special, Air Up). Perform those moves in order, quickly, with no delay.

How do I create my own fighter?

On the select screen, tap "+ Create": pick a base fighter, color, name, and your specials. Your creation joins your roster.

Are there ads?

There's an optional video for coins, never during fights. In the European Economic Area, you'll be asked for consent.

Found a bug? What should I do?

Message us from the Contact section: say what phone you have, the game version, and what you were doing just before.

Is this game kid-friendly?

It's got a cartoon style, doesn't need any account, and doesn't collect your personal info. See the Privacy.
